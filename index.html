About the game

Matter Scatter

In MATTER SCATTER, you play as matter itself. Play through the real timeline of the universe:

Q-G
Quark-Gluon Plasma & Hadronization
Bond quarks into protons and neutrons.
Nuc
Nucleosynthesis
Fuse into an atomic nucleus.
Rec
Recombination
Capture electrons and go molecular.
Dark
Dark Ages
Grow a cloud of hydrogen while dodging a black hole.
Star
Stellar Nucleosynthesis
Climb the fusion ladder from hydrogen to iron inside a living star, and go supernova.
Dust
Stardust & the Protoplanetary Disk
Gather the wreckage of a star into a brand new world.

Every phase has its own hazards — photons, antimatter, gamma rays, rogue black holes. Surviving means actually understanding what you're made of at every step. MATTER SCATTER is based on real physics. Learn it to play it.
