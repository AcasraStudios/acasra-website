This site is a static, informational site. We do not run a contact form, so no message content is stored on any server we control — clicking "Contact" opens your own email client and sends directly to our inbox, governed by whatever email provider you use.

We do not currently run analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies on this site. If that changes (for example, adding privacy-respecting visit analytics), this policy will be updated first and the tool named here.

Standard web server/hosting logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) may be collected automatically by whichever host serves this site, as is standard for essentially all websites — we do not access or use these beyond basic security and uptime troubleshooting.

4. Legal basis and your rights (DPDP Act / GDPR)

Where we do process any personal data (for example, an email you send us), we do so only for the purpose you provided it — responding to you — and only for as long as reasonably needed for that purpose.

Under India's DPDP Act, as a Data Principal you have the right to access information about how your personal data is processed, request correction or erasure of your data, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. If you are in the EU, GDPR gives you equivalent and additional rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). To exercise any of these, email us at the address above and we'll respond as quickly as we reasonably can.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with third parties except where required by law, or where a service we rely on for the site's own operation (such as our hosting provider) necessarily processes it as part of delivering the site to you.

6. Children's data

This site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Matter Scatter itself is rated for a general audience — see its Steam store page for the applicable age rating.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the studio, the site, and applicable law evolve. Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: studios@acasra.com.
